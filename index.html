Daily DBA checklist
  1. Confirm the database and listener/service are up.
  2. Check last night's backup finished and passed verification.
  3. Review free space on data files, logs and disks.
  4. Look for long-running, blocked or failed sessions and jobs.
  5. Scan the error log and alerts for new warnings.
  6. Check replication or standby lag.
Version numbers, paths and passwords are examples. Check the vendor documentation for your release.
