High CPU / Hard Parsing Bottleneck

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: V$SQL & V$SQLAREA

Identifies expensive statements consuming abnormal amounts of CPU run time. Hard parsing indicates that the database engine is spending critical cycles generating execution plans instead of reusing existing ones.

Triage & Resolution Query:

SELECT sql_id, executions, cpu_time/1000000 cpu_sec, elapsed_time/1000000 rows_eval, sql_text \nFROM v$sql \nORDER BY cpu_time DESC \nFETCH FIRST 10 ROWS ONLY;
